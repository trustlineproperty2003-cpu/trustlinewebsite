About This Property

This is a premium 1200 sq.mt industrial and commercial plot located in the heart of Naini's thriving industrial corridor — Prayagraj's most active economic zone. The plot benefits from its strategic position adjacent to key manufacturing units, logistics facilities, and road infrastructure that makes it one of the most sought-after land parcels currently available in the market. The plot features a regular rectangular shape with a 30-metre road-facing frontage, making it highly versatile for a wide range of industrial and commercial applications.

The land has been thoroughly verified with clean title documentation including Khatauni, Khasra, and Registry copies — all in order and free of any legal disputes or encumbrances. Electricity connection with three-phase supply is readily available at the plot boundary. Water supply through both government pipeline and borewell is feasible, and drainage infrastructure is already in place along the roadside. The east-facing orientation ensures excellent natural light and ventilation, ideal for manufacturing shed construction.

Naini is Prayagraj's designated industrial hub under the UP Industrial Development Corporation. With continued government investment in infrastructure, expansion of the UPSIDC industrial estate, and the ongoing development of the Naini-Meja road corridor, land values in this area have appreciated at 12–18% annually over the past five years and are projected to continue on this growth trajectory. Investing in this plot represents not just a business opportunity but a highly secure long-term capital investment in one of UP's most developed industrial zones.

Location & Connectivity

Naini is Prayagraj's primary industrial suburb, located approximately 8 km from Civil Lines on the southern bank of the Yamuna river. The area houses over 400 industrial units ranging from small-scale workshops to large manufacturing facilities, supported by a well-developed road and utility infrastructure. This plot is located within the UPSIDC-notified industrial zone, making it eligible for government subsidies, bank financing, and industrial clearances. Key landmarks nearby include IFFCO plant, Naini Industries Association complex, Naini police station, and the Naini railway station (4 km). Prayagraj Airport is accessible in approximately 25 minutes via Bamrauli.
