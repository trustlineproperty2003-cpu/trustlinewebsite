At TrustLine Properties, we operate with 100% legal transparency and accountability. Every single plot in our portfolio undergoes a certified 30-year title search, Khasra-Khatauni verification, and RERA compliance check. We don't just broker transactions; we handle the complete lifecycle from site inspections to official registry and revenue mutation.
We have active, verified land parcels in prime residential zones (Civil Lines, George Town, Tagore Town), emerging growth corridors (Naini, Jhunsi, Phaphamau), and commercial/agricultural belts around Shankargarh and the Chitrakoot highway route.
Before purchasing, essential documents include the 30-year Title Deed chain, latest Khasra & Khatauni revenue records, Section 143 (Non-Agricultural Land order), Encumbrance Certificate (Bar-e-Kifalat), UP RERA project registration, and Prayagraj Development Authority (PDA) master plan layout approvals. Our legal team verifies all these before listing.
In Uttar Pradesh, the standard stamp duty is 7% for male buyers and 6% for female buyers (calculated on circle rate or agreed transaction value, whichever is higher), along with a 1% government registration fee. Our advisory team provides a 100% transparent cost sheet with zero hidden charges.
